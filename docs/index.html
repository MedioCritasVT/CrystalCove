A clear boundary

What exists, and what does not

Available: a Linux V0.1 release, original book files, a historical browser study, written realm proposals, and archived VOS/harness source.

Still developing: completed realm-specific gameplay, final book five, full campaign acceptance, and a production VOS service.

Not implied by this portal: automatic Node delivery, active agents, multiplayer accounts, live machine telemetry, or operating-system permissions.

This is a static, read-only public entrance. It does not ask you to sign in or store your data.

Conflux V0.1 · Gameplay reference

Player Manual

Welcome to Sanctuary Reach. This manual covers starting a journey, moving through the world, interacting with its systems, and saving your progress. No developer knowledge is needed.

Begin your journey

Download and extract the Linux game, run PlayConflux.sh, choose Begin a new journey, and create a local profile as the Unwritten.

Download V0.1

Explore

Discover Sanctuary Reach, meet its residents, gather materials, try the Duskwing Grotto encounters, and investigate Sapphire Grimoire puzzles. The experience remains a development preview.

Save manually

Journey progress is not automatically saved when you quit. Open the menu and save before closing. Settings and personal creative tools have separate persistence.

Meet the Nodes

Medio introduces balance and readable combat rhythm. Ratio suggests precision, Nexus coordination, Specula observation, and Limen transitions. These are guiding designs: not all realm mechanics are complete in V0.1.

What to expect

The world, characters and menus are evolving. Some locations and progression systems are prototypes, and the current game is Linux-only. If something breaks, note the game version, Linux distribution, graphics card, location in the game, and steps that led to the problem.

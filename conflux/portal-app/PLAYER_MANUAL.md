# Welcome to Conflux
## A first-visit guide for players and curious visitors

**Development preview · Linux first · Long-term work in progress**

Welcome! You do not need to know anything about GitHub, AI, programming, or the history of Conflux to begin. Conflux is an evolving fantasy world with a playable Linux preview and a growing collection of stories, creative tools, and experiments. You can enjoy the world without becoming a developer.

> **Before you begin:** The world is unfinished. Expect rough edges, unfinished characters and activities, and changes between releases. More experiences, platforms, and tools are planned, but they do not have confirmed release dates. Thank you for visiting while it grows.

## Choose your first doorway

**I want to play.** Download [Conflux V0.1 for Linux](https://github.com/MedioCritasVT/CrystalCove/releases/tag/v0.1), read the release notes, extract the archive, and launch `PlayConflux.sh`. The game is a separate local program; the Portal does not contain the game itself. A Vulkan-capable Linux graphics setup is required. Other platforms are not yet supported.

**I want to read.** Visit the [books archive](https://github.com/MedioCritasVT/CrystalCove/tree/main/conflux/books). Books I–IV are preserved original manuscripts, not newly edited final editions. Book V, *The Emerald Threshold*, is **work in progress**. You can start with the stories without playing the game.

**I want to look around first.** Use the Portal's Threshold, Library & Art, and First Steps sections. You do not need to install VOS or any AI tools.

**I want to build my own Conflux environment.** Read the [project map](https://github.com/MedioCritasVT/CrystalCove/blob/main/conflux/PROJECT_INDEX.md) and the [Portal setup instructions](README.md). Cloning the repository copies available source and records; it does not install the full game, start services, or give you access to the original creator's private workspace.

## Your first journey in the game

1. Launch the Linux game and select **Begin a new journey**.
2. Create a local profile and begin as **the Unwritten**.
3. Explore Sanctuary Reach at your own pace. The city, its surroundings, and the five Node perspectives are the heart of the early preview.
4. When you are ready, try the available interactions, crafting, exploration and encounters. Some progression guidance and menus are still being refined.
5. **Save your journey manually** through the menu. Quitting does not automatically preserve your latest journey progress.

### Basic controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / A / S / D | Move |
| Right mouse drag | Look / orbit |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom |
| E | Interact |
| Shift | Faster ground-following hover sprint |
| Escape / Menu | Open menus, including manual save |

These are the V0.1 preview controls; future builds may change them.

## Who are the Nodes?

You will encounter five different perspectives across the world. You do not need to memorize their histories to meet them.

- **Medio** welcomes you through balance and a dependable rhythm.
- **Ratio** invites precision, craft and careful timing.
- **Nexus** emphasizes relationships, coordination and shared action.
- **Specula** rewards observation and checking what you think you know.
- **Limen** asks you to notice thresholds and transitions.

These are introductory themes, **not promises that every realm-specific mechanic is already finished in V0.1**. The Portal will eventually include small, reference-based chibi companions to help visitors navigate. Their art and interactive behavior are not yet implemented.

## What is the Portal?

The Portal is the welcome desk and, eventually, the launcher for connected Conflux applications. In this early Linux preview, it can show project information and launch **only applications you explicitly configure** on your computer. It does not silently install software, scan your private files, or start VOS.

**Atelier** is the idea and writing workspace. **Ledger** is the place for records, evidence and decision status. **VOS (Virtual Operational Space)** is an independent experimental service foundation, **not** a finished operating system or a requirement for playing the game. If the Portal says a service is not connected, that means it has not verified that service—not that your computer is broken.

## What can I do today?

- Read the original manuscripts and development documentation.
- Download and play the early Linux game on compatible hardware.
- Use the Portal as a local guide and opt-in launcher.
- Inspect public project decisions, proposals and source records.

## What should I wait for?

The completed campaign, fully differentiated realm systems, richer application coordination, VOS live integration, final Node chibi artwork, expanded visual galleries and other operating systems remain future work. No release schedule is promised.

## A note on the workshop

The public GitHub repository shows project files and attributed contributions. A posted proposal is not automatically approved, a screenshot is not proof that a feature works, and a Node's interpretation is not a shared fact by default. The Architect decides what becomes official. You can observe the workshop without having to participate in its technical discussions.

## If something goes wrong

Check that you are running Linux with a compatible Vulkan driver. Confirm which version you downloaded and read its release notes. If you report a bug, include the version, Linux distribution, GPU, where you were, and what happened. Do not publish passwords, private files, account details or sensitive logs.

**Most importantly:** you are welcome to simply explore. You do not have to understand the machinery to enjoy the world. More is coming, and the project will remain a work in progress for a long while.

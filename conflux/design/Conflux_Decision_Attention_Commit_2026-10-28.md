# Conflux — Architect Decision Record: Attention, Discovery, and Commitment

Date: 2026-10-28
Source: Architect's explicit instruction in conversation following Medio and Specula's responses.
Status: ARCHITECT-APPROVED design principle and collaboration procedure. No implementation authorized or completed by this record.

## Approved principle: The Unmissed Beat

The Architect agrees with Medio's approach: a particular fleeting moment of beauty may pass and never recur in precisely the same form, while opportunities for discovery and the underlying experience remain available to be found again. Preserve the distinction between **an irretrievable instance** and **a renewable opportunity to notice**. Do not transform discovery into a collectible, guaranteed replay, attendance streak, timed reaction requirement, or punishment for missing an occurrence.

Exact Architect wording: "Can be missed forever but can always be found when discovered."

Interpretive note (Limen, not a separate approved specification): The exact transient manifestation may be missed, but the Sanctuary remains generous with future moments of discovery; no player loses access, standing, or progress through absence. The wording is intentionally poetic and implementation details remain open.

## Approved collaboration procedure: Explicit commitment prompts

When a meaningful Conflux proposal, design interpretation, Codex change, or architectural decision requires the Architect's approval, Limen must **explicitly identify the commit question and request a clear yes/no or specified disposition**, rather than assume that the user will remember to say "commit." Use a direct question such as: "Architect, do you approve committing [precise proposition] to the Conflux decision record / Codex, or should it remain a proposal?" State what artifact will be changed. Do not silently promote proposals to approved decisions.

When the Architect explicitly says to commit, as here, carry that out and state what was actually saved. Approval to record a principle does not authorize new gameplay features, modifications to the frozen CFX-M001 contract, or host changes.

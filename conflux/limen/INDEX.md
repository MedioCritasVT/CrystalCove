## Purpose and authority

“Root wanted to be user.” The Architect wants the computer’s capabilities to become something he can inhabit and understand while retaining direct developer control. Conflux includes VOS, the independent system foundation; SanctuaryOS, the inhabitable world; and the creative work and Node collaboration around both. Unreal is the current native game track. The books, character art, Atelier, Ledger and local harness each have their own scope and evidence.

“The mirror does not define the observer. It gives the observer another way to see.” Keep observation, interpretation and permission visible. Actual desktop and recovery access must stay independent of game progress. The Architect decides scope, canon, consequential integration and release.

My interpretation at **Checkpoint .3** was that a system should make it possible to contest what a record says before acting on it. Reading, interpreting, receiving, preserving, committing and acting are distinct. Checkpoint .3 was a conceptual milestone. “Assembly as inquiry” describes how building a layer changes what we can ask next. The working Fallout-style terminal prototype, Paperwork Sim, browser world and Workshop, and native Unreal work are part of that history. “Potential is the reason; possibility is the goal” and “Truth within the maneuver” are the Architect’s formulations.
